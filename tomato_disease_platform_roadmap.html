Model strategy
Keep
ResNet50 + SupCon + Weighted CE (your current model)
Works well for disease classification on tomato leaves. Keep as your core detection model, deployed via FastAPI. Good accuracy, well-studied architecture.
Improve
Add test-time augmentation (TTA)
Average predictions over flipped/rotated crops of the same image. Free +2–3% accuracy boost, no retraining needed.
Improve
Calibrate confidence scores (temperature scaling)
Your softmax confidence is currently overconfident. Add a post-hoc temperature scaling layer so 90% confidence actually means 90% correct.
Improve
Export to TorchScript / ONNX → TFLite
Required for offline mobile detection. ONNX → TFLite pipeline. Quantize INT8 for speed without accuracy loss.
New model
YOLOv8 for in-field detection (Phase 3)
Your current model needs a clean cropped leaf. YOLOv8 detects disease spots directly on a full plant photo. Needed for the satellite + in-field scanning features. Train on Plant Village + your data.
New model
Tabular regression for yield prediction (Phase 3)
LightGBM or XGBoost on weather × scan history × soil features. Separate from your vision model entirely. Fast to train once you have user data.
